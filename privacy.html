Nib’s Little Kitchen — Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026.

This policy explains the privacy practices of Nib’s Little Kitchen, a children’s cooking game. The publisher’s name appears on the app’s store listing.

What the game does

The game does not require a Nib account. It has no advertising, behavioral tracking, chat, camera, microphone recording or location features. The publisher does not operate a game analytics service or add a separate analytics SDK. The game does not ask children for names, email addresses, dates of birth or other identifiers.

The grown-ups reading challenge is processed on the device. Its answer is not saved as a user profile or sent to a server.

Local kitchens and preferences

Saved kitchens and preferences, including sound settings, are stored locally on the device. We do not operate a cloud-save service. Ordinary cooking does not require a network connection.

Deleting the app or clearing its data can delete the local kitchen and preferences. Restoring a store purchase restores access to purchased content; it does not recover a saved kitchen.

Purchases and store services

The optional, permanent Full Kitchen purchase is processed by Apple or Google Play. Their store services may process account, payment and technical information under their own policies to provide and protect billing. We do not receive payment card details or the user’s store password.

The native purchase integration checks signed store transactions or receipts on the device and uses purchase and ownership information to unlock content, restore access and keep verified ownership available offline. The game does not send purchase records to a publisher-operated server.

Purchases and restoration require access to the relevant store services. Store purchase records follow Apple’s or Google’s own policies and retention rules.

Separate public support and privacy pages

Our separate public support and privacy pages use GitHub Pages. When you visit a GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, including visits made without signing into GitHub. GitHub handles that information under its own privacy statement.

The publisher adds no website analytics, scripts, forms or gameplay to these public pages. The public site does not host the game’s code, assets or saved kitchens. Reading this bundled policy inside the app does not load the public website.

Voluntary support email

If you choose to contact support, the information you include is handled in the publisher’s support mailbox and by its email provider. Please have a parent or guardian contact support and avoid sending a child’s personal information.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to answer and resolve support requests or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the contact below; information may still need to be retained for those purposes.

Deleting data and managing purchases

Use your device’s app-data controls or delete the app to remove its local game data. The game has no Nib account to delete.

Deleting local data does not cancel store ownership or refund a purchase. Manage payment questions and refund requests through the store where the purchase was made. Apple and Google purchases are separate.

Policy updates and contact

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies this version.

For privacy questions, support or a request about support correspondence, contact the publisher: idiego612@gmail.com.
